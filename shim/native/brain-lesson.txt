Infection tactics: a zombie and a human are different players. As a ZOMBIE the walker now hunts the nearest human -- one with other humans within 6 units counts as farther, so the lonely ones go first -- and charges straight in with a light weave whatever its caution, the crowd or its shield say: no cover, no recharge, no break-off, no backing off (a zombie only scores at sword range; the bad-window caution used to halve its charge). As a HUMAN it walks to the biggest group of humans within 80 units and holds with them (kiting swords and hammers as before; no "stuck" grenade while it holds on purpose). It switches the moment it is infected ("[inf] I'm a ZOMBIE now" / "I'm a HUMAN"). Expect kills as a zombie to rise and lone-human deaths to fall.
