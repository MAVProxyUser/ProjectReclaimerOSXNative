Recording

Archived: every voice packet the server sends this client — every channel you can hear (global, team, proximity), muted players included — with who said it (name, team, ids), plus every chat line, join / leave, kill and server message. Voice from channels this client doesn't receive (e.g. the other team's team chat) never reaches this computer.
Voice on a public server is transmitted to everyone in the channel it is sent on (global, team or proximity); once you have joined, your client is one of the recipients it is addressed to — like a scanner tuned to talk groups, it keeps what it was sent. Recording what you receive as a participant is allowed in one-party-consent places such as Ohio, so the box is ticked by default. Some places require every party's consent, and whether in-game voice counts as a private conversation there can vary: untick it there, and only who talked when is kept, no audio.
