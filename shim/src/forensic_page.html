Recording

Archived: every voice packet the server sends this client — every channel you can hear (global, team, proximity), muted players included — with who said it (name, team, ids), plus every chat line, join / leave, kill and server message. Voice from channels this client doesn't receive (e.g. the other team's team chat) never reaches this computer.
Voice on a public server is transmitted to everyone in the channel it is sent on (global, team or proximity); once you have joined, your client is one of the recipients it is addressed to — like a scanner tuned to talk groups, it keeps what it was sent. Where one-party consent applies, a participant may record what they receive, so the box is ticked by default. Where every party must consent, untick it: only who talked when is kept, no audio. Players can be anywhere, and a place that requires everyone's consent may apply its law to its own residents. Not legal advice; laws change.
Where one-party consent applies
United States — one-party: federal law (18 U.S.C. § 2511(2)(d)), District of Columbia, Alabama, Alaska, Arizona, Arkansas, Colorado, Georgia, Hawaii, Idaho, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Kentucky, Louisiana, Maine, Minnesota, Mississippi, Missouri, Nebraska, New Jersey, New Mexico, New York, North Carolina, North Dakota, Ohio, Oklahoma, Rhode Island, South Carolina, South Dakota, Tennessee, Texas, Utah, Vermont (no statute), Virginia, West Virginia, Wisconsin, Wyoming. Mixed: Connecticut (one-party in person, all-party for calls), Michigan (participants may record their own conversations), Nevada (one-party in person, all-party for calls), Oregon (one-party for calls, all-party in person). Every party: California, Delaware, Florida, Illinois, Maryland, Massachusetts, Montana, New Hampshire, Pennsylvania, Washington.
Elsewhere — commonly treated as one-party / a participant may record: Canada, the United Kingdom, Ireland, New Zealand, Israel, South Africa, Japan, the Netherlands, Denmark, Sweden, Norway, Finland, Italy, Spain, Brazil, India. Australia differs by state (Victoria, Queensland and the Northern Territory one-party; others stricter). Stricter (recording a private conversation without everyone's consent is an offence): e.g. Germany, France, Switzerland, Austria.
